# Listening-Game mini-scene ideas (backlog for review)

The Listening Game now runs **mini-scenes** — a theme label + a visible prompt + 4
within-scene choices, bookended by Pip (see `src/data/gameScenes.js`). Two scenes are
anchored on classic songs (Old MacDonald, Head-Shoulders-Knees-&-Toes), which is the
strongest hook. Below are more scenes to consider — the song-anchored ones first.

**Shipped (4):** Old MacDonald’s Farm · Snack Time · At the Park · Head to Toes.

## Buildable NOW (existing art + clips — only 2 song-line clips per scene needed)

| Scene | Song hook | Items (all exist) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Zoo / Safari** | *We’re Going to the Zoo* | Lion*, Elephant*, Monkey*, Bear*, Zebra*, Wolf* | Wild-animal counterpart to Farm — **all have real sounds** (the roar/etc. is the wow). Strongest next pick. |
| **Dance Party** | *If You’re Happy and You Know It* | Clapping, Dancing, Jumping, Waving, Hugging, Laughing | Pure-verb scene, joyful; "clap your hands / stomp your feet". |
| **Fruits & Veggies (Market)** | *(chant)* | Apple, Banana, Avocado, Broccoli, Carrot, Cucumber | Splits the food set off from Snack; great colour/variety. |
| **Morning Routine** | *This Is the Way* | Waking up, Brushing teeth, Getting dressed, Washing hands, Eating | Verb scene mirroring the "Every Day with Pip" routine, in find-it form. |
| **1-2-3 Counting** | *Five Little Ducks / 1,2,3* | One–Five (premium number cards) | Find-the-number by name; different flavour but works. Pair with the ducks. |
| **Birds & Bugs** | *(garden chant)* | Bird*, Owl*, Crow*, Bee*, Frog*, Butterfly, Turtle | Small nature scene; several real sounds. |

`* = plays a real recorded animal sound.`

## Open questions for review
- **Session mix:** keep Farm always-in, or rotate all scenes evenly once there are 6–8?
- **Scene length:** 4–5 finds each feels right; revisit if we add longer scenes.
- **Prompt verb:** currently "Where’s the …?" / "Who’s …?" (matches audio). Some scenes
  might read nicer as "Find the …" — but the on-screen text must stay identical to the clip.
- **New worlds vs. reuse:** Zoo/Dance/Market/Morning are all buildable today from Safari +
  Things-I-Do + Home Village — no new content world needed.

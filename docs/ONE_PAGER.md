## HERO PANEL

# First words, out loud.
### A gentle, sound-first app that helps toddlers *hear* and *say* their first words.

Real illustrations. A warm human voice. Real animal sounds. No scores, no ads,
no pressure — just curiosity and play.

*Made for little ones who are just finding their voice — including late talkers
and speech-therapy homework.*

## WHY PARENTS LOVE IT (benefit panel)

- **Speech-first, always.** Every word is spoken in a warm, real-sounding voice —
  never a robotic phone voice. Your child *hears* the word, then tries it.
- **Now says their name.** TinyVoice greets and cheers your child **by name** in
  that same warm voice.
- **No scores. No streaks. No pressure.** Learning is a gentle collection and a
  little celebration — it rewards curiosity, not "winning."
- **Real pictures, real sounds.** Hand-crafted illustrations and true recorded
  animal sounds — an elephant really trumpets.
- **Grown-up-safe.** No ads, no in-app nagging, no data sold. Works offline once
  loaded. Installs like a real app on any phone or tablet.
- **Two kids? Twin Mode.** Gentle turn-taking and a shared "you did it together!"
  finish — no rivalry.

## WHAT'S INSIDE (feature panel)

- **7 worlds, 140 words** — My Body · Things I Do · Home Village · Safari Island ·
  Rainbow Island · Counting Mountain · Music Forest.
- **Learning screen** — a picture, the word, a big "hear it" button, and a
  Language Ladder that grows from single words to short phrases.
- **Listening games** — "Where's the dog?" with the real sound; a right tap earns
  warm, labelled praise. Wrong taps are *never* a fail — help gently escalates.
- **Word Board (AAC)** — a real communication board with stable picture positions,
  for children who point before they speak.
- **Every Day with Pip** — calm routine scenes (mealtime, bath, bedtime) that model
  everyday language. **[SCREENSHOT: routines.png — the Every Day with Pip picker]**
- **Sing with Pip** — 13 classic sing-along songs with a karaoke caption.
- **Parent dashboard** — a gentle weekly note ("This week Ava loved Safari Island"),
  plus voice, wait-time, and focus-word settings — optional cloud backup.

## HOW TO USE IT — in 5 easy steps (how-to panel)

1. **Open it** — visit the link or install it to your home screen (Add to Home
   Screen). It works offline afterward.
2. **Set up your child** — tell it how many children (1 or 2) and type each name.
   You can rename anytime.
3. **Pick a voice** — choose one of three warm storyteller voices in the parent
   area. Your child's name is spoken in that voice.
4. **Play together** — tap a world, hear the word, say it back, celebrate. Let
   curiosity lead — there's no wrong path.
5. **Peek at the parent dashboard** — a calm weekly summary, plus set a daily
   screen-time limit and "wait time" so your child has room to respond.

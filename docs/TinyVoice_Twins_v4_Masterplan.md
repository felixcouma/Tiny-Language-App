# TinyVoice Twins v4
## Speech Expansion & Early Language Development Masterplan

### Designed for Audrey & Adriel

---

# Vision

TinyVoice Twins is not designed to teach words.

It is designed to help children progress through the natural language ladder:

1. Sound Recognition
2. Word Recognition
3. Word Production
4. Two-Word Combinations
5. Three-Word Combinations
6. Simple Sentences
7. Real World Communication

---

# Why This Version Is Different

## Improvements Over Previous Versions

### Speech Therapy Alignment
- Focuses on 2–3 word combinations.
- Supports expressive language development.
- Encourages spontaneous speech.
- Builds receptive and expressive language simultaneously.

### Twin-Specific Design
- Designed for Audrey and Adriel.
- Supports turn-taking.
- Encourages sibling interaction.
- Reinforces social communication.

### Real-World Learning
- Real photos.
- Real sounds.
- Real objects from daily life.
- Real communication opportunities.

# Learning Framework

Every item follows this sequence:

## Stage A — Listen

"Cow"

"Moooo"

Purpose:
- Auditory recognition

## Stage B — Point

"Where is the cow?"

Purpose:
- Receptive language

## Stage C — Repeat

"Can you say cow?"

Purpose:
- Expressive language

## Stage D — Expand

"Brown cow"

"Big cow"

Purpose:
- Two-word combinations

## Stage E — Build

"Big brown cow"

"Cow eats grass"

Purpose:
- Three-word combinations

## Stage F — Communicate

"What color is the cow?"

"What is the cow doing?"

Purpose:
- Conversation development

# Twin Mode

Unique feature.

Round 1:
"Audrey, find the cow."

Round 2:
"Adriel, find the duck."

Round 3:
"Can both of you find something blue?"

Benefits:
- Turn taking
- Listening
- Social communication
- Shared learning

---

# Living World Progression

Replace static category cards.

Each category becomes a living world.

### Safari Island
Animals appear as they learn.

### Rainbow Island
Colors paint the environment.

### Counting Mountain
Numbers unlock pathways.

### Home Village
Objects populate the village.

### Music Forest
New sounds awaken creatures.

Improvement:
Progress feels meaningful without rewards, coins, streaks, or pressure.
